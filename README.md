# Data Engineering Notes: A Practical Guide for Data Analysts who wants to make a switch to Data Engineer and Data Engineers

If you're a **Data Analyst looking to transition into Data
Engineering**, or a **Data Engineer who wants to revise important
fundamentals**, these notes are intended to help you build and refresh
your knowledge across key data concepts.

I've organized the resources into five areas:

## 1. Data Architecture Notes

Understand how data systems and pipelines fit together, from source
systems to data consumption.

## 2. Data Modeling Notes

Learn how data is structured to support analytics, reporting, and
downstream applications.

## 3. Data Warehousing Notes

Revise the concepts behind storing, organizing, transforming, and
serving analytical data.

## 4. Data Definition Notes

Review general data topics

## 5. Data Governance Notes

Understand how organizations manage data responsibly and consistently.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Who are these notes for?

-   **Data Analysts** who want to understand the concepts involved in
    moving toward a Data Engineering role.
-   **Aspiring Data Engineers** who want to strengthen their foundations
    before working on production pipelines.
-   **Working Data Engineers** who want a quick refresher on important
    concepts.
-   **Anyone preparing for data-related interviews** who wants to revise
    these topics in an organized way.

## How to use these notes

Use them as a starting point for learning and revision. For interview
preparation or practical experience, pair the concepts with hands-on
exercises

I hope these resources help you learn, revise, and connect the concepts.
If you have suggestions for other foundational topics worth adding, feel
free to share them.

Note - This is the notes I used while preparing for interviews

Happy learning!
