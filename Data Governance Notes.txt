1 - What is Data Governance?

Data Governance is the set of processes, rules, roles, and controls that ensure data is accurate, secure, consistent, usable, and trusted.

In simple words:
It is how an organisation manages data so that everyone uses the same, correct, secure data.

2 - Why is Data Governance Important?

1 - Better decision-making
2 - Improved data quality
3 - High trust in reports
4 - Security & compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, RBI, etc.)
5 - Avoids duplicate, inconsistent or missing data
6 - Reduces cost and risk

3 - Core Components of Data Governance

1. Data Policies

High-level rules that decide how data should be managed.
Example: "Customer PII must be encrypted and not shared externally."

2. Data Standards

Detailed formats or rules.
Example: Dates must be in YYYY-MM-DD.

3. Data Procedures & Processes

Step-by-step instructions for implementation.
Example: Process for onboarding new datasets into the warehouse.

13 - Data Quality Dimensions (Top 7)

1. Accuracy:

Data is correct.
Example: Customer age = 28 (not 280).

2. Completeness:

No missing values.

3. Consistency:

Same data across systems.
(ERP shows price = 100, CRM shows = 100 → consistent)

4. Timeliness:

Data updated at right frequency.

5. Validity:

Follows business rules.
Example: Email must contain @

6. Uniqueness:

No duplicates.

7. Integrity:

Relationships between tables remain strong (FK constraints).

14 - Data Governance vs Data Management

✅ Top Collibra Interview Questions & Answers (Clear & Practical)

1 . What is Collibra?

Answer:
Collibra is a Data Governance and Data Intelligence platform that helps organizations manage data assets, maintain data quality, define data ownership, create business glossaries, and ensure compliance.

Key features:

 Business Glossary
 Data Catalog
 Data Lineage
 Data Stewardship workflows
 Data Quality integration
 Policy management

2.What are the core components of Collibra?

Answer:
The main components are:

 Domains – categories of assets (e.g., Business Terms, Data Sets, Reports).
 Assets – individual items (e.g., Customer Table, Order Report).
 Attributes – metadata details about an asset.
 Relations – connections between assets (e.g., Table contains Column).
 Workflows – approval and governance processes.
 Communities & Domains – structure for organizing ownership.

3.What is a Business Glossary in Collibra?

Answer:
A Business Glossary contains standardized business definitions to ensure everyone in the organization speaks the same language.

Example:

 Customer → A person who purchases products from the company.

It reduces ambiguity and aligns business & technical teams.

4.What is a Data Dictionary in Collibra?

Answer:
A Data Dictionary stores technical metadata such as tables, columns, schemas, data types, constraints, etc.

Difference from Business Glossary:

 Glossary = Business definitions
 Data Dictionary = Technical details

5.What is a Steward in Collibra?

Answer:
A Data Steward is responsible for:

 Maintaining accurate metadata
 Approving business terms
 Handling data quality and access issues
 Running governance workflows

6.What are Workflows in Collibra?

Answer:
Workflows are automated processes like:

 Approving business terms
 Requesting data access
 Creating new assets
 Managing changes

They are built using BPMN (Business Process Model & Notation).
